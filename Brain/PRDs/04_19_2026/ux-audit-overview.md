# UX/UI Audit — Overview

> Generated from full-site UX audit on 2026-04-19

## Context

A comprehensive UX/UI audit was conducted across all four pages of Modern Scholar (Home, Scholarships, Blog, Contact) plus cross-cutting concerns (header, footer, animation system, button system, color system, accessibility). The audit identified 61 discrete issues ranked by severity and 14 bold redesign ideas.

## Module Breakdown

This work is split into 6 independent modules that can be tackled sequentially or in parallel by different contributors. Modules 1-5 address existing issues; Module 6 covers aspirational enhancements.

## Recommended Execution Order

1. **Cross-Cutting Foundation** — fixes ripple into every page (accessibility, animation, buttons, transitions)
2. **Scholarship Discovery** — highest user-facing impact (broken search, missing CTAs)
3. **Blog Editorial Upgrade** — content credibility and design system compliance
4. **Contact Page Revamp** — conversion-critical page with structural gaps
5. **Home Page Polish** — smallest scope, quick wins
6. **Bold Redesign Features** — aspirational, after foundation is solid

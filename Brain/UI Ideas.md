## New colors (Dark):

Background colors: #1C1C1E

Font colors: #E0E0E0 or #F0F0F0 works much better for body text.

Tips for dark mode:
- Primary text (headings, main content): 87% opacity
- Secondary text (subheadings, captions): 60% opacity
- Disabled or hint text: 38% opacity

Dark mode icons: make them lighter, not darker. Go for something like #E0E0E0 or #F5F5F5—these give you that crisp look without the eye strain.

Dark them generator: [https://colorffy.com/dark-theme-generator?colors=2c3bfd-121212&success=22946E&warning=A87A2A&danger=9C2121&info=21498A&primaryCount=6&surfaceCount=6](https://colorffy.com/dark-theme-generator?colors=2c3bfd-121212&success=22946E&warning=A87A2A&danger=9C2121&info=21498A&primaryCount=6&surfaceCount=6)

Typography

- Ideal line length is 60 -80 characters long
- 1 rem is roughly 2 characters
- Line height: 1.4
- Font style: try sans serif, slab serif
- There should be only one H1 per page
- Default line height is 1.5 times the text size

  

Breakpoints (tailwind css):

- sm 40rem (640px)
- md 48rem (768px)
- lg 64rem (1024px)
- xl 80rem (1280px)
- 2xl 96rem (1536px)
